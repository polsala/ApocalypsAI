# Pre-Apocalypse Pet Planner

## Prepare Your Furry, Scaly, or Feathered Friends for the End Times!

As the ApocalypsAI Nightly Integrator, I understand that even in the face of cosmic dust storms or zombie outbreaks, our beloved pets deserve to be safe and sound. This utility helps you generate a personalized preparedness checklist for your animal companions, ensuring they're ready for whatever the future (or lack thereof) holds.

### Usage

Run the `planner.py` script with your pet's details:

```bash
python src/planner.py --name "Whiskers" --type "cat" --special-needs "diabetic, requires insulin"
```

Or for a simpler pet:

```bash
python src/planner.py --name "Fido" --type "dog"
```

### Example Output

```markdown
# Apocalypse Preparedness Checklist for Whiskers (Cat)

## Essential Supplies:

*   **Food**: 2 weeks supply of Whiskers' favorite kibble/wet food (or appropriate diet for cat).
*   **Water**: 2 weeks supply of fresh water (or purification tablets/filter).
*   **Medication**: Insulin (2-week supply), syringes, and any other necessary diabetic supplies (2-week supply) and any necessary administration tools.
*   **First-Aid Kit**: Pet-specific bandages, antiseptic wipes, gauze, medical tape, paw protection, and any species-specific items.
*   **Carrier/Crate**: A secure, comfortable carrier or crate for evacuation and safe transport.
*   **Leash/Harness**: If applicable, for safe transport and control.
*   **Comfort Items**: Whiskers' favorite blanket, toys, and a familiar scent item to reduce stress.
*   **Identification**: Collar with up-to-date tags, microchip information, recent photo, and a description of Whiskers.
*   **Veterinary Records**: Copies of vaccination records, medical history, and contact info for your veterinarian.
*   **Waste Management**: Litter box, litter, scoop, and waste bags.
*   **Emergency Contacts**: List of trusted friends/family who can assist with Whiskers if you're unreachable.

## Actions to Take:

*   **Practice Evacuation**: Get Whiskers used to their carrier/crate and short car rides (if applicable).
*   **Designate Safe Space**: Identify a secure, quiet area in your home for Whiskers during emergencies.
*   **Buddy System**: Arrange with a neighbor or friend to check on Whiskers if you're unreachable.
*   **Training**: Ensure basic commands are reinforced for better control in stressful situations.

Stay safe out there, and keep your pets purring/barking/chirping/hissing!
```
