# Gloom-and-Doom Affirmations Generator

## Overview
Feeling a bit down about the impending doom? Or perhaps the doom has already arrived, and you just need a pick-me-up that understands your unique predicament? The `gloom-and-doom-affirmations-generator` is here to help! This whimsical utility provides daily affirmations tailored for the discerning survivor, blending a touch of dark humor with just enough 'optimism' to keep you going. Because even when the world is ending, your spirit doesn't have to.

## How to Use

1.  **Navigate to the utility directory:**
    ```bash
    cd utils/gloom-and-doom-affirmations-generator
    ```

2.  **Run the affirmation generator:**
    ```bash
    python3 src/affirmations.py
    ```

    The utility will print a random affirmation to your console.
