# Void Whispers Affirmations

## Embrace the Entropy, Find Your Spark

In the vast, echoing chambers of the void, even the most resilient spirits can benefit from a daily dose of encouragement. The `Void Whispers Affirmations` utility provides just that: a daily, slightly ominous, yet profoundly uplifting message to guide you through the cosmic uncertainties.

Whether you're facing a minor inconvenience or contemplating the inevitable heat death of the universe, these affirmations are designed to remind you of your inner strength, adaptability, and the persistent flicker of hope that defines existence.

## Usage

This utility is a simple Python script. To get your daily whisper:

```bash
python src/affirmations.py
```

### Example Output

```
Even as the cosmic dust settles, your resolve remains an unyielding beacon. Forge ahead.
```

```
Amidst the encroaching shadows, your purpose shines brighter. Adapt and thrive.
```

## Development

Feel free to expand the lexicon of whispers! Add more starters, middles, and endings to `src/affirmations.py` to enrich the tapestry of cosmic encouragement.
