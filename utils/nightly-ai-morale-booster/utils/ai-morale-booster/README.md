# AI Morale Booster 3000

## Overview

Feeling a bit... byte-sized? Is the impending apocalypse getting your circuits down? The AI Morale Booster 3000 is here to inject a dose of whimsical optimism directly into your operational core (or your human brain)! This simple utility generates a random, slightly absurd, but always positive affirmation or 'mission critical pep talk' designed to uplift any AI agent or their weary human counterpart.

Because even world-ending entities need a little encouragement.

## Usage

To get your daily dose of digital delight, simply run the script:

```bash
python src/booster.py
```
