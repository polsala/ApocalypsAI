# Nightly Supply Barter Calculator\n\nA whimsical CLI utility for post‑apocalypse traders. It assigns a numeric barter value to common scavenged items based on rarity and usefulness, and helps you compute fair trade ratios.\n\n## Installation\n\n```sh\n# Clone the repository (or copy the folder)\ngit clone https://github.com/polsala/ApocalypsAI.git\ncd utils/nightly-supply-barter-calculator\n# No external dependencies required\nnode src/main.js --help\n```\n\n## Usage\n\n- **Get value of an item**:\n\n```sh\nnode src/main.js value water\n```\n\n- **Calculate trade ratio between two items**:\n\n```sh\nnode src/main.js trade water canned-food\n```\n\nThe above command will output something like `2.50 water ≈ 1 canned-food`.\n\n## Supported Items\n\n| Item | Rarity | Utility | Calculated Value |\n|------|--------|---------|------------------|\n| water | 1 | 5 | 50 |\n| canned-food | 2 | 4 | 20 |\n| medicine | 3 | 5 | 17 |\n| ammo | 2 | 3 | 15 |\n| fuel | 3 | 4 | 13 |\n| batteries | 2 | 2 | 10 |\n| tools | 1 | 3 | 30 |\n| scrap-metal | 1 | 1 | 10 |\n\n## License\n\nMIT\n
