# Nightly Emoji Decoder\n\n## Overview\n\n`nightly-emoji-decoder` is a tiny Node.js utility that translates a sequence of fruit and vegetable emojis into plain‑text letters. It uses a fixed, whimsical mapping (🍎 → a, 🍌 → b, …) and is perfect for secret‑message puzzles, fun chat games, or just adding a dash of post‑apocalyptic flair to your communications.\n\n## Installation\n\n1. Clone the repository (or copy the `src/` folder).\n2. Ensure you have Node.js (v14+) installed.\n3. No external dependencies are required.\n\n## Usage\n\n```sh\nnode src/index.js "🍎🍌🍒"\n# => abc\n```\n\nYou can also pass emojis with spaces – the utility will strip whitespace automatically:\n\n```sh\nnode src/index.js "🍎 🍌 🍒"\n# => abc\n```\n\n## Mapping Table\n\n| Emoji | Letter |\n|-------|--------|\n| 🍎 | a |\n| 🍌 | b |\n| 🍒 | c |\n| 🍇 | d |\n| 🍉 | e |\n| 🍓 | f |\n| 🍑 | g |\n| 🍍 | h |\n| 🥝 | i |\n| 🍅 | j |\n| 🥑 | k |\n| 🍆 | l |\n| 🥔 | m |\n| 🥕 | n |\n| 🌽 | o |\n| 🌶️ | p |\n| 🧄 | q |\n| 🧅 | r |\n| 🍄 | s |\n| 🥜 | t |\n| 🍞 | u |\n| 🥐 | v |\n| 🥖 | w |\n| 🧀 | x |\n| 🍖 | y |\n| 🍗 | z |\n\nAny emoji not present in the table will be translated to a `?` placeholder.\n\n## Testing\n\nRun the bundled tests with Node:\n\n```sh\nnode tests/test_index.js\n```\n\nAll tests should pass, confirming correct decoding logic.\n
