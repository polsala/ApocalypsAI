# Nightly Quantum Entanglement Simulator

A whimsical web-based quantum entanglement simulator that visualizes the spooky action at a distance. Watch as particles become entangled and observe how measuring one instantly affects its partner, no matter how far apart they are!

## Features

- Interactive quantum particle visualization
- Real-time entanglement simulation
- Measurement effects with instant state collapse
- Educational tooltips explaining quantum concepts
- Whimsical animations and sound effects

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
2. Click "Create Entangled Pair" to generate two particles
3. Drag particles to separate them
4. Click on a particle to measure it
5. Watch as its entangled partner instantly collapses to the opposite state

## Technologies

- HTML5 Canvas for rendering
- Vanilla JavaScript for simulation logic
- CSS3 animations for smooth interactions
- Web Audio API for quantum sound effects

## Educational Value

This simulator demonstrates:
- Quantum superposition (particles exist in multiple states until measured)
- Quantum entanglement (particles become linked regardless of distance)
- Wave function collapse (measurement forces a definite state)
- Non-locality (instantaneous effects across distance)

## Fun Facts

- The "spooky action at a distance" quote is from Einstein
- Quantum entanglement is used in real quantum computing
- This simulator is simplified for educational purposes
- In reality, quantum states are much more complex than just "up" or "down"
