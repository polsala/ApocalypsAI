# Nightly Quantum Entanglement Simulator

A whimsical web-based quantum entanglement simulator that visualizes the spooky action at a distance in real-time. Perfect for understanding quantum mechanics concepts or just watching pretty particles dance!

## Features

- **Real-time quantum simulation** with entangled particle pairs
- **Interactive particle manipulation** - drag particles to see entanglement effects
- **Measurement visualization** - click particles to collapse their quantum state
- **Educational tooltips** explaining quantum concepts
- **Customizable simulation parameters** (entanglement strength, decoherence rate)
- **Export functionality** for sharing simulation states

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser
2. Watch entangled particles dance and interact
3. Drag particles to manipulate their positions
4. Click particles to measure/collapse their quantum state
5. Adjust simulation parameters in the control panel
6. Export interesting states to share with friends

## Technical Details

- Built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas
- Uses requestAnimationFrame for smooth 60fps rendering
- Implements simplified quantum mechanics principles
- No external dependencies required
- Mobile-friendly responsive design

## Educational Value

This simulator demonstrates key quantum concepts:
- **Superposition**: Particles exist in multiple states until measured
- **Entanglement**: Particles remain connected regardless of distance
- **Measurement collapse**: Observing a particle forces it into a definite state
- **Decoherence**: Interaction with environment breaks quantum effects
