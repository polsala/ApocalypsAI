# Nightly Quantum Entanglement Simulator

A whimsical web-based quantum circuit simulator with entanglement visualization for educational purposes. Built with React and Three.js for an interactive quantum computing experience.

## Features

- **Interactive Quantum Circuit Builder**: Drag and drop quantum gates to build circuits
- **Real-time Entanglement Visualization**: See quantum states and entanglement in 3D
- **Educational Tooltips**: Learn about quantum gates and principles
- **State Vector Display**: View the mathematical representation of quantum states
- **Measurement Simulation**: Observe quantum collapse and probability distributions

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Drag quantum gates from the sidebar onto the circuit grid
3. Connect gates to qubits to build your quantum circuit
4. Click "Simulate" to see the entanglement visualization
5. Use "Measure" to observe quantum collapse

## Quantum Gates Available

- **Hadamard (H)**: Creates superposition
- **Pauli-X (X)**: Quantum NOT gate
- **Pauli-Y (Y)**: Y-axis rotation
- **Pauli-Z (Z)**: Z-axis rotation
- **CNOT**: Creates entanglement between qubits
- **Phase (S)**: Adds phase to quantum state
- **T Gate**: π/4 phase rotation

## Educational Value

This tool helps visualize:
- Quantum superposition

- Quantum gate operations
