🧐 Welcome to Your Thought-Pod! 🧐

This is your personal, ephemeral sanctuary for brilliant ideas, quick notes, and isolated prototyping.

Drop your .html, .txt, or .md files into the src/thoughts/ directory and access them directly via your browser.

For example, if you create src/thoughts/my-idea.html, you can view it at http://localhost:8080/my-idea.html.

Happy thinking! May your ideas be ever-brilliant.
